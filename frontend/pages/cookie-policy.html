01 / The basics

Introduction

This policy explains how the PetSathi website uses cookies and similar browser technologies. It reflects the website's current frontend implementation and should be read alongside any notices shown on the site.

02 / A simple explanation

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can ask your browser to save. They can help a website remember information between visits. Similar technologies, such as browser storage, can also save information on your device but work differently from cookies.

03 / Common categories

Types of Cookies

Essential

Help a site deliver core functions, such as secure navigation or a session. PetSathi's reviewed frontend does not intentionally set essential cookies.

Functional

Remember choices or progress. PetSathi uses browser storage for some page features; this is similar technology, not a cookie.

Analytics

Measure how visitors use a site. No analytics or advertising tracking scripts were found in the reviewed frontend.

Advertising & other

May be used by a site or third party to deliver targeted content. PetSathi does not currently implement advertising or marketing trackers.

04 / PetSathi today

How PetSathi Uses Cookies

We found no code in the reviewed frontend that intentionally sets or reads HTTP cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers. Some features use browser storage instead:

  • Local storage: saves checklist progress on certain bird and rabbit care pages, and handover information for the handover feature, on your device.
  • Session storage: keeps notification-page state in your browser session.

This information is stored by your browser for those features; it is not a cookie. The details may change if the website's features change.

05 / External resources

Third-Party Cookies & Services

Some pages request resources from Google Fonts, cdnjs (for Font Awesome icons), and image hosts such as Unsplash. These providers receive requests when those resources load and handle them under their own privacy practices. The reviewed frontend does not use these resources as analytics or advertising trackers. We have not verified whether a provider's infrastructure sets cookies, so please refer to that provider's current privacy and cookie information for details.

06 / Your controls

Cookie Management

You can view, remove, or block cookies using your browser's privacy or site settings. Browser help pages explain how to manage cookies for your particular device.

You can also clear or restrict local and session storage through your browser's site-data settings. If you do, saved checklists, handover entries, or notification state may be removed or may no longer be saved. PetSathi does not currently provide a separate cookie-preference panel because the reviewed frontend does not intentionally set cookies.

07 / Staying current

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when the website or its use of browser technologies changes. The current version will be published on this page; please check here for updates.
