Frequently Asked Species Questions

Expert answers to guide responsible species selection and community tank stocking.

Yes, in a sufficiently large planted tank (10+ gallons), a male Betta can peacefully coexist with calm bottom-dwellers like Corydoras catfish, peaceful snails, or small schooling tetras that do not nip fins. Avoid fast fin-nippers (like Tiger Barbs) or fish with long colorful fins (like fancy Guppies) which can trigger territorial attacks.
Goldfish are temperate coldwater fish requiring unheated water between 18–22°C (65–72°F), high oxygen saturation, and heavy mechanical filtration. Tropical fish require heated water between 24–28°C. Keeping goldfish in warm tropical water dangerously speeds up their metabolism and shortens their lifespan.
Schooling fish require a bare minimum of 6 individuals, though 10 to 12 is optimal. In smaller numbers, schooling fish feel vulnerable, stressed, and may exhibit fearful hiding or unusual fin-nipping aggression toward tank mates.
